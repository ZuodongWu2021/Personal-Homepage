Biography

Zuodong Wu was born in Zhumadian, Henan Province, China. He is a Ph.D. student in Cyberspace Security at the School of Cyberspace Security, Beijing Jiaotong University. His research interests include privacy computing and applied cryptography. In 2024, he joined Muroran Institute of Technology, Hokkaido, Japan, as a CSC-sponsored visiting Ph.D. student, under the supervision of Prof. Mianxiong Dong and Prof. Kaoru Ota.

During his Ph.D. study, he has been conducting research on cyberspace security, privacy protection, authorized privacy-preserving data retrieval, decentralized identity, and applied cryptographic protocols. He has published four first-author papers in leading international journals, including IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing, IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management, Computer Networks, and Computer Standards & Interfaces.

His research focuses on decentralized identity framework design, privacy-preserving authorized sharing of personal data, anonymous authorization mechanisms, and cryptographic privacy-preserving protocol design. He has also developed several prototype systems. He has participated in multiple research projects, including the National Key R&D Program of China on blockchain-based Web 3.0 frontier technologies, the National Natural Science Foundation of China project on secure and controllable theories and methods for blockchain, and the Henan Provincial Science and Technology Innovation Talent Program on privacy protection methods for content-centric networking.
